About Deccan AI
Deccan AI is a fast-growing, venture-backed AI infrastructure company focused on training, evaluating, and improving next-generation AI systems. Headquartered in the Bay Area, with a growing India hub in Hyderabad, the company was founded by alumni of IIT Bombay, IIM Ahmedabad, and former Google leaders.
We work with some of the worlds leading AI frontier labs and research organizations, including Google DeepMind, Snowflake, and other cutting-edge AI teams. Backed by Prosus Ventures, Deccan AI recently raised $25M in Series A funding and is entering a significant growth phase.
With a global network of over 1 million experts, advanced automation systems, and vertically integrated platforms, we deliver the high-quality data and evaluation infrastructure that state-of-the-art AI models depend on. As the AI infrastructure market rapidly expands, Deccan AI is building the systems powering the future of AI.

About the Role
Were hiring an ML Engineer to work on Robotics, partnering directly with a frontier AI lab. Youll work on embodied AI perception, manipulation, and navigation systems that bridge the gap between simulation and the real world.

What Youll Do
Build perception systems for robotic manipulation and navigation
Develop sim-to-real pipelines to transfer learned policies from simulation to physical systems
Work on motion planning, trajectory optimization, and imitation learning
Collaborate with hardware and controls teams on real-world robot deployment
Debug and iterate on models running on physical or simulated robotic systems
What Were Looking For
2-4 years of hands-on experience in robotics, perception, or embodied AI
Strong Python and/or C++ skills
Familiarity with ROS/ROS2, MuJoCo, Isaac Sim/Isaac Gym, Gazebo, or PyBullet
Understanding of core robotics concepts: SLAM, kinematics, grasping, motion planning
Bonus: experience with sim-to-real transfer, imitation learning, or humanoid/manipulation systems
Why Join
Direct exposure to frontier-lab-scale embodied AI problems
Work alongside top ML research and engineering talent
High-ownership role with real technical depth
