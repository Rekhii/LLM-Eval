Role Overview

We are looking for an AI/ML Engineer with 5 years of experience, including 3+ years of hands-on experience in Agentic AI OR LLM/Generative AI.

The engineer will work with technical and project teams to design, develop, integrate, and deploy AI/ML solutions. The role requires strong Python and software engineering skills, along with practical experience building AI-powered applications.

Key Responsibilities

Design and develop AI/ML solutions and applications using Python.
Build and integrate LLM/Generative AI applications or AI agent solutions based on project requirements.
Develop RAG-based applications, prompt workflows, and LLM integrations where required.
Build AI agents using frameworks such as LangGraph, LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, or PydanticAI.
Integrate AI solutions with REST APIs, databases, and existing enterprise applications.
Develop and maintain scalable AI services using FastAPI or similar frameworks.
Deploy and support AI/ML applications on Azure and/or Databricks.
Work with development and platform teams on CI/CD, testing, deployment, monitoring, and production support.
Maintain clean, reusable, and well-documented code using Git and standard development practices.
Understand project requirements and work with technical teams to deliver practical AI solutions.
Required Skills

5 years of experience in AI/ML, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, or related software engineering roles.
3+ years of hands-on experience in Agentic AI OR LLM/Generative AI.
Strong Python development experience.
Practical experience with LLMs, RAG, prompt engineering, or AI agents.
Experience with at least one framework such as LangGraph, LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, or PydanticAI.
Experience with REST APIs and application integration.
Familiarity with Azure and/or Databricks.
Experience with Git and CI/CD practices.
Good communication and problem-solving skills.
Good to Have

Experience with both Agentic AI and LLM/Generative AI.
Exposure to MLOps/LLMOps and AI application monitoring.
Experience with Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Search, vector databases, MLflow, or LangSmith.
Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, or event-driven architectures.
Experience using AI-assisted development tools such as GitHub Copilot, Windsurf, or Codeium.
Education

Bachelor's or master's degree in computer science , AI/ML, Data Science, Engineering, or a related technical field.
