About Deccan AI
Deccan AI is a fast-growing, venture-backed AI infrastructure company focused on training, evaluating, and improving next-generation AI systems. Headquartered in the Bay Area, with a growing India hub in Hyderabad, the company was founded by alumni of IIT Bombay, IIM Ahmedabad, and former Google leaders.
We work with some of the worlds leading AI frontier labs and research organizations, including Google DeepMind, Snowflake, and other cutting-edge AI teams. Backed by Prosus Ventures, Deccan AI recently raised $25M in Series A funding and is entering a significant growth phase.
With a global network of over 1 million experts, advanced automation systems, and vertically integrated platforms, we deliver the high-quality data and evaluation infrastructure that state-of-the-art AI models depend on. As the AI infrastructure market rapidly expands, Deccan AI is building the systems powering the future of AI.

About the Role
Were hiring an ML Engineer to work on Speech & Vision systems, partnering directly with a frontier AI lab. Youll build multimodal models that see, hear, and understand working on real perception and generation problems at the cutting edge of computer vision and speech AI.

What Youll Do
Build and optimize computer vision models for detection, segmentation, and recognition tasks
Develop speech pipelines spanning ASR (speech-to-text), TTS (text-to-speech), and speaker diarization
Work on multimodal models that combine vision, audio, and text understanding
Experiment with diffusion models and generative approaches for vision/audio tasks
Optimize models for real-world latency, accuracy, and deployment constraints
What Were Looking For
2-4 years of hands-on experience in computer vision, speech processing, or multimodal ML
Strong Python skills and experience with PyTorch/TensorFlow
Familiarity with OpenCV, YOLO, Detectron2, Whisper, Kaldi, ESPnet, or similar tools
Solid understanding of core CV/speech concepts: object detection, segmentation, OCR, ASR/TTS pipelines
Bonus: experience with diffusion models, GANs, or voice cloning systems
Why Join
Direct exposure to frontier-lab-scale multimodal AI problems
Work alongside top ML research and engineering talent
High-ownership role with real technical depth
